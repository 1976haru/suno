Written clear in your own hand

Waiting can make a minute heavy
Waiting can polish faith
Then a truck slowed by the roses
Its white side flashed like promise
And joy stood at the gate

[Chorus]

Into one small cry

[Final Chorus]

A speck of dust, a turning wheel
I hold my breath and smile for real
With summer in my eyes
I let the whole porch hear me
Softly now, with both hands shaking
Here Comes Your Letter
